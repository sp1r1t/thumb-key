Suave is a fully customizable, free and open-source Android keyboard with a T9 poweruser soul.

The core is the engine: layouts are data, gestures are recognized and dispatched on purpose, and the map stays stable enough to learn by feel - touch-typing on a phone instead of hunting and pecking. Meta keys and RAW IME are first-class for terminals and other apps that expect real key events.

Build any layout you want on that engine: T9-style pads, a Hacker Keyboard-style board (planned), even a Copy/Paste-only utility layout. S12 is the default Suave letter layout on top; it is not the ceiling.

Features:
- Customizable layout data and gesture engine
- Large keys and directional swipes for muscle memory
- Meta keys and RAW IME for hacker / terminal use
- Local clipboard history (optional)
- Themes and appearance controls
- No account, no telemetry baked into the product identity
