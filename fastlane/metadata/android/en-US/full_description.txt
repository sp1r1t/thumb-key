Suave is a privacy-conscious Android keyboard built around large keys and directional swipes.

S12 is the flagship layout. Typing uses predictable key positions and swipe gestures instead of cloud-backed prediction.

Features:
- Large keys sized for thumbs
- Directional swipes for less common characters
- Local clipboard history (optional)
- Themes and appearance controls
- No account, no telemetry baked into the product identity

Suave is libre software under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0. It grew out of a Thumb-Key fork and now ships its own keyboard engine.
